Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 4, 2026 · Effective Immediately

At WebUtils, one of our highest priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information collected and recorded by WebUtils and how we handle and protect it.

Summary: WebUtils is engineered with a strict local-first architecture. Tools process your text, passwords, images, and calculations locally inside your browser. We do not store or transmit your sensitive tool inputs to our servers.

1. Local-First Client-Side Data Processing

Unlike traditional web applications where files and text are uploaded to a remote server for processing, WebUtils operates primarily within your client device:

  • Text & Code Processing: JSON formatting, Markdown rendering, regex evaluation, and diffing occur in browser JavaScript memory.
  • Cryptography & Passwords: Hash algorithms (MD5, SHA-256, HMAC), password generation, and encryption run via the standard browser window.crypto API.
  • Media & Images: Image compression, cropping, and color extraction utilize the HTML5 Canvas API locally.
  • Network Exceptions: A small subset of network testing tools (e.g., DNS lookup, HTTP requester, IP geolocation) make direct outbound requests to the specific endpoints chosen or clearly labeled on the page.

2. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising

We may partner with third-party advertising vendors, including Google AdSense, to serve advertisements when you visit our website. These companies may use cookies and web beacons to collect non-personally identifiable information about your visits to this and other websites in order to provide advertisements about goods and services of interest to you:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to WebUtils or other websites on the internet.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve targeted ads to users based on their browsing behavior across the web.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Alternatively, you can opt out of third-party vendor cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info or Your Online Choices.

3. Cookies and Browser LocalStorage

WebUtils does not use tracking cookies for first-party profiling. We utilize standard browser localStorage strictly for user experience features:

  • Theme Preference: Storing whether you prefer dark mode or light mode (localStorage.getItem('theme')).
  • Favorite Tools: Saving tools you have bookmarked with the star icon for quick access on your device.
  • Recent Tools: Maintaining a temporary list of your recently viewed tools in your browser.

You can clear or disable LocalStorage and cookies at any time via your browser settings.

4. Log Files

Like most website hosting platforms, WebUtils follows standard procedure using server log files. The information collected by log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, referring/exit pages, and click counts. These logs are not linked to any information that is personally identifiable and are used solely for analyzing trends, administering the site, and defending against malicious cyberattacks.

5. CCPA Privacy Rights (Do Not Sell My Personal Information)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), California consumers have the right to:

  • Request disclosure of categories and specific pieces of personal data collected.
  • Request deletion of consumer personal data.
  • Request that a business that sells personal data not sell their personal data.

Because WebUtils does not collect or sell personal consumer data, we are compliant by design.

6. GDPR Data Protection Rights

We want to ensure you are fully aware of all of your data protection rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

  • The right to access: You have the right to request copies of your personal data.
  • The right to rectification: You have the right to request correction of any inaccurate information.
  • The right to erasure: You have the right to request that we erase your personal data.
  • The right to restrict or object to processing: You have the right to object to our processing of your personal data.

7. Children's Privacy

Protecting children while using the internet is especially important to us. WebUtils does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that your child provided personal information on our website, please contact us immediately.
