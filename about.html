ABOUT WEBUTILS

Empowering Productivity, Protecting Privacy

Over 1,000+ browser-based utilities crafted for developers, writers, designers, and students.

🎯 Our Mission

At WebUtils, our mission is simple: provide instant access to high-performance, everyday digital tools without requiring account sign-ups, software installations, or compromising your personal data. Whether you need to format complex JSON strings, convert units, compress an image, or calculate a mortgage payment, WebUtils delivers results directly in your browser.

Local-First Guarantee: 99% of formatting, calculation, and generation tasks execute entirely client-side using JavaScript, Web Workers, and Web Crypto APIs. Your sensitive input data never leaves your device.

⚡ Core Principles

  • Zero Server Storage: Your inputs, files, code snippets, and calculations stay safely in your browser memory. We do not store, log, or sell your private data.
  • No Installations: Everything runs immediately inside modern browsers (Chrome, Edge, Safari, Firefox) on desktop PCs, tablets, and smartphones.
  • Transparent Boundaries: Whenever a tool requires an external network connection (such as DNS queries or external API testing), we make that network dependency explicit.
  • Lightning-Fast Performance: Built with clean, optimized HTML5, CSS3, and native JavaScript to ensure near-zero load times and responsive interfaces.

🛠️ What We Offer

Our expansive toolkit covers 35 distinct categories, including:

  • Developer Utilities: JSON/YAML formatters, Base64 & URL encoders, Regex testers, JWT decoders, and code beautifiers.
  • Generators: Secure passwords, UUID/NanoID, QR codes, Barcodes, and mock datasets.
  • Media & Graphics: Image compression, SVG tools, color format converters, and CSS gradients.
  • Everyday Calculators: Financial loans, mortgages, percentages, BMI, and time zone converters.
  • Writing & Text: Word counters, diff checkers, case converters, and Markdown previewers.
